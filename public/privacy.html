Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

কৃষি এআই ("Krishi AI", "the app") is a free agricultural assistant for farmers in Bangladesh. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. Using the app means you accept this policy.

1. Summary

  • Your photos stay on your device unless you explicitly enable Cloud Sync.
  • No account required. We do not ask for your name, phone, or email to use the app.
  • No ads. No data selling. No third-party tracking.
  • Analytics are optional and cookieless, and are off unless you turn them on.

2. Data we process

  • Photos you upload for AI diagnosis. They are sent to the Google Gemini API only when you request an analysis, and stored locally in your browser.
  • Local app data — chat history, scan results, your profile (name, district, crops, farm size). This is stored in your browser's localStorage and never uploaded unless you enable Cloud Sync.
  • District — used to look up local weather and severe-weather alerts. It is sent to Open-Meteo, a free weather API, to fetch a forecast. We do not request precise GPS location.
  • Usage counts — a small anonymous counter of actions (for example, "3 diagnoses today") used to manage the AI fair-use budget. No identifiers are attached.

3. What we do NOT collect

  • Your name, email address, or phone number (unless you type it into your own profile).
  • Precise GPS coordinates.
  • Advertising identifiers, device fingerprints, or cross-app tracking identifiers.
  • Your raw voice recordings — speech recognition is processed by your device's own engine.

4. Third-party services

  • Google Gemini — processes the image and prompt you submit for a diagnosis. Google's terms apply.
  • OpenRouter — fallback text model when Gemini is unavailable (free tier).
  • Open-Meteo — free weather data, no API key.
  • Google Fonts — the Hind Siliguri typeface.
  • Your own Cloud Sync server (optional) — if you enable it, your reports and images go to a server that you or your community hosts. We have no access to it.

5. Optional analytics

If you enable usage statistics (Profile → Settings), the app sends anonymous events such as "a tool was opened" to a Plausible-compatible server you choose. These events contain no personal data, set no cookies, and are never used for advertising. The app also respects your browser's Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control settings — if either is enabled, nothing is sent at all.

6. Your controls

  • Clear all local data: Profile → "ডেটা মুছুন" (Delete data), or clear browser storage / uninstall.
  • Turn off analytics: Profile → Settings → usage statistics toggle.
  • Turn off weather alerts: use the bell icon → notification toggle.
  • Revoke notification permission from your device settings.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

8. Changes

If this policy changes materially, the "Last updated" date above will change and the app will show an in-app notice.

9. Contact

Questions or deletion requests: privacy@krishi-ai.app. We aim to respond within 7 days.
